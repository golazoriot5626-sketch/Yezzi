How to Play Yezzi

🧩 Drag & Snap: Drag pieces onto the board grid or near matching neighbors. Pieces automatically snap into position when placed correctly!

🔗 Connected Groups: Matching adjacent pieces lock together into groups and can be dragged around as one unit.

👁️ Ghost Preview: Toggle the target background image preview with the Eye button.

🖼️ Edge Pieces: Use the Frame button to show only border pieces when starting large puzzles.

🔍 Pan & Zoom: Scroll wheel or pinch gestures allow zooming into intricate details.
